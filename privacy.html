Your privacy matters to me. This Privacy Policy explains how Flynn James Pontino ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit my website or engage my professional services.

🔒 My Commitment: I will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your trust is my most valuable asset.

1. Information I Collect

I collect information that you voluntarily provide, as well as certain technical data automatically when you use my website.

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title when you fill out the contact form.
  • Communication Data: Information you share via email, phone, or video consultations.
  • Service Data: Project details, business requirements, and engagement-related information.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent on the website.
  • Cookies: Minimal cookies for theme preference and session management.

2. How I Use Your Information

I use your information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To respond to inquiries, provide consulting services, and fulfill contractual obligations.
  • Communication: To send important updates, respond to questions, and provide information you request.
  • Improvement: To analyze website usage and improve my content, services, and user experience.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

I process your personal information based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: When you have given explicit consent for specific purposes.
  • Contract: When processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you.
  • Legitimate Interests: When processing is necessary for my legitimate business interests, provided your rights don't override these interests.
  • Legal Obligation: When processing is required to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Protection

I take data security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted during transmission using SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel have access to your information.
  • Secure Storage: Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access.
  • Regular Reviews: Security practices are reviewed and updated regularly.

5. Data Retention

I retain your personal information only as long as necessary:

  • Contact Information: Retained for the duration of our professional relationship plus 2 years.
  • Service Records: Retained for 5 years for legal, tax, and audit purposes.
  • Website Analytics: Retained for 12 months for performance analysis.
  • Communication Records: Retained for 3 years for reference and continuity.

6. Sharing Your Information

I do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. I may share your data with trusted service providers:

  • EmailJS: For contact form email delivery. View their privacy policy.
  • Hosting Providers: For website hosting and data storage.
  • Communication Tools: For email and video consultations.

📋 All third-party providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and comply with data protection laws.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and other applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information I hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations).
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time without affecting previous processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me using the information in Section 12.

8. Cookies

I use minimal cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Theme Preference: To remember your dark/light mode preference.
  • Session Management: For basic session functionality.
  • Analytics: For website performance analysis (anonymized).

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

My services are not directed at children under 13 years of age. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact me immediately to have it removed.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the Philippines, where I am based. These countries may have different data protection laws. I ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

I reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. I encourage you to review this page periodically.
